## POSITIONING (Short paragraph)

AskQ is a conversational intelligence platform that sits on top of your existing enterprise systems — SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, or any ERP — and lets leadership teams ask business questions in plain language. Instead of navigating modules, waiting for reports, or depending on analysts, users simply ask. AskQ responds with structured, contextual, role-aware insights — not raw data, but reasoning.

---

## THE PROBLEM

Enterprises run on powerful systems that generate massive data — but extracting meaning still requires analysts, reports, dashboards, and days of waiting. Executives ask critical questions like *"Why is fulfillment declining?"* or *"Where is working capital blocked?"* — and the answer sits buried across systems that respond with numbers, not reasoning. The gap between data and decisions is costing enterprises time, clarity, and competitive advantage.

---

## THE SOLUTION

AskQ connects to your enterprise data sources, interprets the schema, understands the business context of your question, and delivers structured intelligence in seconds. It provides explanations, root cause analysis, and actionable summaries — tailored to the role of the person asking. No SQL. No BI tools. No waiting. Just ask.

---

## KEY DIFFERENTIATORS

**Domain-Agnostic**
Works across manufacturing, retail, banking, SaaS — any industry. Adapts to your data, not predefined templates.

**ERP & Database Neutral**
Connects to SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, PostgreSQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, legacy systems, and more. Replaces nothing. Sits on top of everything.

**Role-Based Intelligence**
A COO gets operational insights. A CFO gets financial exposure analysis. A Procurement Head gets vendor performance. Same data, different intelligence — shaped by who is asking.

**Reasoning, Not Reporting**
AskQ doesn't return tables and charts. It returns structured answers that explain *why* something is happening and *what* to do about it.
